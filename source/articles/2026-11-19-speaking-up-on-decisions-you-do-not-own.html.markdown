A few rules I try to follow when I disagree with a decision that is not mine to make.

* Being right about something doesn't make it your job to drive it.
* Say it clearly once, bring new evidence if you get it, then let the owner decide.
* Raising a concern needs a reasonable doubt, not certainty.
* Own your area; advise everywhere else.
* Letting people learn is not the same as staying silent while they fail.
